GRC Copilot

Automated evidence collection, control gap analysis, and evidence-grounded questionnaire drafting for SOC 2 and ISO 27001.

What it demonstrates

A compact Python proof of concept that collects source-system evidence, hashes and timestamps it, maps it to controls, and produces audit-ready gap reports and questionnaire drafts with citations.

Impact

Shows how manual screenshot-based compliance work can become a repeatable, verifiable evidence pipeline where stale, missing, and tampered evidence are surfaced as control findings.
